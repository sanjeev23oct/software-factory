On the road in three commands

1

Pick a route

Copy the nearest sample and point it at your repository.

python3 factory/flow.py init shop \
  --sample backend-api \
  --repo ~/code/shop
2

Redraw it

Set your tracker, your rules and your tests. Move the approvals.

python3 factory/flow.py --target shop show
python3 factory/flow.py --target shop validate
3

Send one issue

It runs until your first approval stop.

python3 factory/flow.py --target shop \
  start --issue 42
python3 factory/flow.py --target shop run
